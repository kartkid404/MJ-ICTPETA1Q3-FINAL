Chocolate Hills

Behold the enchanting landscape of over 1,200 limestone hills in Bohol, shaped like chocolate drops when the grass turns brown during the dry season. The Chocolate Hills are a geological wonder shrouded in local legends, offering panoramic views and unique photo opportunities. Visit the viewing deck, ride an ATV around the hills, and explore nearby caves. It's a sweet escape like no other!
